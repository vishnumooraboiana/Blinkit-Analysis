**KEY FEATURES**

Interactive Dashboards:
Multiple dashboards for Sales Overview, Product Performance, Customer Analysis, City-wise Performance, and Profitability — all designed with intuitive slicers, drill-downs, and tooltips for easy exploration.

**Dynamic Filters:**
Analyze data dynamically using filters for Date, City, Product Category, Customer Type, and Discount Range to view performance from different business perspectives.

**Custom Calculations:**
Created calculated measures using DAX to track key business metrics:

Total Sales = SUM(Sales)

Total Profit = SUM(Profit)

Profit Margin (%) = (Profit / Sales) × 100

Average Order Value (AOV) = Sales / Total Orders

Repeat Customer Rate (%) = (Repeat Orders / Total Orders) × 100

**Comparative Analysis:**
Enabled comparison of cities, product categories, and time periods using trend lines, side-by-side visuals, and KPI indicators to identify high- and low-performing segments.

Discount Impact Analysis:
Built visuals to analyze the relationship between discounts, sales volume, and profit, helping identify optimal discount levels and loss-making scenarios.

Data Storytelling:
Designed visually clear and business-focused dashboards that highlight sales growth trends, customer behavior patterns, and profitability drivers, making insights easy to understand for stakeholders.

Clean & Professional Design:
Applied Power BI best practices with consistent color themes, conditional formatting, readable labels, and logical layout structure for executive-level reporting.
